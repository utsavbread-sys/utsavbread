Our Story

50+

Years of Legacy

For over 50 years, our family has been deeply involved in Kota's bread industry, supplying some of the region's most trusted brands and building expertise rooted in real experience, not classrooms.

This five-decade legacy of quality, discipline, and commitment forms the foundation on which Utsav Bread stands today.

Established in 2024, Utsav Bread Manufacturing began its own production journey, carrying forward the same dedication into every loaf, rusk, and pav — ensuring freshness, hygiene, and consistent quality crafted through years of experience.

"We are not just building a brand; we are carrying forward a legacy of trust."
